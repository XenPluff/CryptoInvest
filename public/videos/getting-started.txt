GUIDE 01 / ACCOUNT SETUP
Your first steps.
Start with the account setup checklist. Keep your login and authenticator information private.

01 / REGISTER
Create your account.
Choose Create an account. Enter your name, email and a unique password, then review the terms.

02 / CHECK YOUR INBOX
Verify your email.
Open the verification link sent to your email, then sign in. Check spam if the message is missing.

03 / ACCOUNT SETTINGS
Complete identity review.
Open Account settings and submit the required identity details. Wait for an approved KYC review.

04 / AUTHENTICATOR SETUP
Add another layer.
Investor two-factor authentication is optional. Enable it in Account settings for codes at sign-in and withdrawal requests.

05 / UNDERSTAND THE PROCESS
Know before you proceed.
Deposits require verified email and enabled platform funding; identity review is optional for deposits. Investments and withdrawals require identity approval. Investor authenticator enrollment is optional.

Digital assets involve substantial risk, including loss of your entire investment.
