GUIDE 03 / WITHDRAWALS
Plan your next step.
Open Withdraw funds. Requests require approved identity, verified email, an authenticator and enabled funding.

01 / AVAILABLE BALANCE
Check what is available.
Enter an amount within your available USD balance. Active investment principal and target estimates are not available funds.

02 / ASSET AND NETWORK
Confirm your destination.
Select the supported asset and network. Enter your external wallet address and check every detail.

03 / AUTHENTICATOR CONFIRMATION
Review, then request.
Enter your current six-digit authenticator code, confirm the destination details and choose Request withdrawal.

04 / REQUEST STATUS
Pending is under review.
The requested amount is reserved while the request is pending. Approval alone does not send cryptocurrency.

05 / TRANSACTION HISTORY
Track the outcome.
Check transaction activity for the outcome and payment reference when recorded. Contact support if you need help.
