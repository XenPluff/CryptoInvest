Understand the deposit process

Ambient instrumental music; no spoken narration. All instructions appear on screen.

GUIDE 02 / DEPOSITS
A careful beginning.
Open Deposit funds. Complete account checks first, and confirm deposits are open before sending anything.

01 / AMOUNT AND CURRENCY
Set your amount.
Enter your USD deposit amount and select an available cryptocurrency. Review the coin amount calculated from the current market price.

02 / CHECK BOTH SIDES
Match the network.
Your sending wallet must use the exact selected network. USDT on TRC20 and ERC20 are different routes.

03 / YOUR PAYMENT WINDOW
Proceed to payment.
Choose Proceed to payment. Copy the receiving address and exact coin amount, or scan the address QR code and enter the amount separately.

04 / YOUR SENDING WALLET
Send the exact amount.
Use the matching network and allow for wallet fees so the recipient receives the amount shown. The coin amount stays fixed; USD credit is confirmed during review.

05 / REPORT YOUR TRANSFER
Return and choose Done.
Return to the payment window, confirm you sent the transfer and choose Done. Your saved payment instructions identify the report. No authenticator code is needed for deposits.

06 / TRANSACTION HISTORY
Follow the review.
Track the pending deposit in activity. Submitting a report does not credit your balance; the operator must verify actual receipt and approve the deposit.

Digital assets involve substantial risk, including loss of your entire investment.
