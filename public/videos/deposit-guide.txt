Understand the deposit process

Ambient instrumental music; no spoken narration. All instructions appear on screen.

GUIDE 02 / DEPOSITS
A careful beginning.
Open Deposit funds. Complete account checks first, and confirm deposits are open before sending anything.

01 / CHOOSE YOUR CURRENCY
Choose your asset.
Choose a currently supported asset in the workspace. Available options are shown on the deposit page.

02 / CHECK BOTH SIDES
Match the network.
Your sending wallet must use the exact selected network. USDT on TRC20 and ERC20 are different routes.

03 / USE THE LIVE WORKSPACE
Verify the destination.
Copy the current address or scan its QR code in your account. Recheck the complete destination in your sending wallet.

04 / YOUR SENDING WALLET
Keep the reference.
If you choose to transfer, allow for network fees. Keep the blockchain transaction hash. Transfers can be irreversible.

05 / CONFIRM YOUR TRANSFER
Submit for verification.
Enter the declared USD value, transaction hash and current authenticator code. Confirm the details and submit.

06 / TRANSACTION HISTORY
Follow the review.
Track the pending deposit in activity. A hash alone never credits your balance; the operator must verify and approve it.

Digital assets involve substantial risk, including loss of your entire investment.
