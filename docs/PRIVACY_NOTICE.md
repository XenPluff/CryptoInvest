## Copy boundaries

The owner supplied a replacement notice asserting an operating LLC and
institutional data collection, custodians, bank partners, AUDU issuance,
automated screening, multi-signature governance and a minimum five-year
retention program. Earlier discussion established that the entity and AUDU
arrangements are proposed. No new verification of those operations or a
controller address, jurisdiction or appointed DPO was supplied.

The published text therefore follows the supplied seven-section structure
while describing the actual application and conditional future requirements:

- AUREON Group LLC is the intended entity name; controller details remain to
  be published. Branding/copyright uses AUREON without asserting incorporation.
- Current account/identity fields, password hashes, investment/payment records,
  communication records and pre-launch card preferences are described.
- Future corporate/beneficial-owner collection, document images, banking
  information and AUDU records depend on the associated services and updated
  notices; the 25% beneficial-owner threshold is preserved where applicable.
- Manual identity review is distinguished from planned automated screening.
- Current hosting/database/email processing, Brevo, operator receiving wallets
  and outstanding processor/international-transfer disclosures remain explicit.
- No connected custodian, bank or card issuer is asserted. Card applications
  do not issue cards, trigger credit checks or automatically share with issuers.
- Actual hashing, HTTP-only sessions and role-based access are distinguished
  from controls requiring verified operational configuration. No new encryption,
  multi-signature, backup or monitoring implementation is implied.
- Five-year retention is a proposed framework subject to law, with detailed
  schedules still to be published. Card withdrawal/history and lawful retention
  limits on deletion remain explained.
- Cookies/preferences, adult-only scope, consent withdrawal, rights, support
  requests and pending supervisory-authority/complaint details remain covered.
  The support route is not presented as an appointed DPO.

The requested no-sale/rental/trade statement is included as the operator's
privacy commitment. The notice does not certify legal compliance. Translations
cover all seven additional site languages and are provided for convenience.
No backend data collection, provider integration, retention setting, financial
operation or production record was changed by this presentation update.
