# AML and identity policy presentation

The AML page uses a three-section formal document covering legal disclosures,
AML/CTF requirements and intellectual property, with the supplied identifier
`AUR-AML-2026.V1`. A shared gold frame, centered brand/title, generous topic and
paragraph spacing, and copyright/version footer support desktop, mobile, RTL,
and dark/light themes. This presentation is a policy document, not a compliance
certificate or regulatory approval. The document heading reads “AML & Identity
Policy”; the requested “Legal & Disclosures,” prefix and framework-status label
are omitted. The AML document uses 16px (12pt) body/topic text, 18px section
headings and a 22px document title, with a 1.65 body line height and compact
paragraph spacing. These sizes are scoped to the AML document; the surrounding
site and risk document retain their existing typography. The AML panel fills
the available viewport width inside the site’s responsive margins, with legal
navigation displayed as wrapping links above the document. Terms and privacy retain the 740px article cap and sidebar layout; the risk
disclosure also uses the wide presentation.

## Copy and operating scope

The requested replacement included assertions of active automated KYC, EDD,
sanctions screening, transaction monitoring and five-year archiving. Those
systems are not established by the current application or supplied records.
The implementation therefore describes a **planned policy framework** and
states current access separately. Clarification and supporting records were
requested; no external providers, registrations or operating processes were
inferred.

- Current identity submissions receive manual operator review of metadata.
- Email-verified investors can submit deposits without identity approval.
  Investments and withdrawals retain approved-identity requirements.
- Document collection, accredited-investor checks, EDD escalation, sanctions
  lists, transaction monitoring, reporting and retention are policy requirements
  to implement, rather than claims of active integrations.
- Reporting and account restrictions depend on applicable law and authorized
  personnel; the document does not assert control over external smart contracts.
- AUREON Group LLC remains an intended entity name; trademark registrations are
  described as actions to pursue. Copyright is attributed to the AUREON brand,
  without asserting an established LLC or registered marks.
- The no-insurance paragraph names FDIC and SIPC without claiming coverage.
- No backend gate, verification status, receiving wallet, ledger record,
  financial setting, retention job or screening integration is changed.

The former intro note and initial-policy banner remain absent. Terms, privacy,
and risk keep their existing content and notice behavior. Risk and AML share
[the formal document stylesheet](../components/public/formal-legal.module.css).
