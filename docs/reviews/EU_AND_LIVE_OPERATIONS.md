# EU and already-launched offering: counsel scoping addendum

This is an intake and review plan, not a legal opinion or a confirmation of
registration, licensing, compliance, or entitlement to accept investors.
Company-specific intake and evidence belong in a private reviewer channel.

## Home jurisdiction and investor markets

Identify the specific EU member state, registered entity and competent national
authority. Distinguish company incorporation, tax/business registration, and any
actual regulated permission; establish the holder, activity, territory, customer
class, conditions and current status of each authorization. Verify through the
relevant official register using the legal entity's identifiers.

An intention to serve retail investors worldwide requires a scoped market plan.
Have counsel evaluate each proposed country and investor category, including
marketing into that country, rather than recording one worldwide approval.
Ask for a practical permitted-country list, restrictions and the controls needed
to enforce those conclusions. No territorial controls have been added by this
coordination work, and no particular country is assumed legally available.

## Regulatory classification before document drafting

The analysis needs to start with the economic arrangement, not its crypto label.
Ask counsel to evaluate whether the investor interest or service is a financial
instrument, fund/collective investment arrangement, regulated investment
activity, cryptoasset service, or another regulated product/activity. Check the
relevant interaction of MiCA, securities/investment-services rules, fund rules,
national implementation and consumer/promotion obligations. Do not treat a
cryptoasset registration or a risk disclaimer as approval of an investment plan.

Counsel should assess the actual rights, return-generating assets and strategy,
investor money flows, issuer/operator/custodian roles, pooled versus segregated
management, redemption terms, target-return substantiation and referral conduct.
The legal brief lists the source parameters and the absence of an implemented
strategy/execution engine; the owner must supply actual business evidence.

## Urgent work if operations have started

Request a prompt legal triage call covering current marketing, registrations,
countries served, terms accepted, actual deposits, holdings, liabilities and
withdrawal requests. Separate the launch of a public website from the acceptance
of real investor assets. Establish the relevant dates and volumes from evidence.

Preserve existing agreement and disclosure versions, advertisements, consent
records, investor/transaction/ledger records, custody and payment evidence,
administrator decisions and audit logs. Do not backdate approvals or documents,
delete records, or invent a prior authorization. Preserve sensitive records
privately; avoid placing investor data or legal advice in this public repository.

Ask counsel for immediate advice on new solicitation/onboarding/deposits, handling
existing investors and withdrawals, communications and any required regulatory
steps. Changes to live funding, customer rights, refunds, transfers or regulator
submissions require the owner's authorized operational decision and appropriate
professional advice. This review preparation itself makes no such change.

Security testing should use a matching staging build with synthetic data under
signed rules of engagement. Urgency does not authorize attacks on investor data,
real wallets, production balances or third-party providers.

## Official starting sources for counsel

The following ESMA pages were retrieved on October 20, 2026:

- [Markets in Crypto-Assets Regulation](https://www.esma.europa.eu/esmas-activities/digital-finance-and-innovation/markets-crypto-assets-regulation-mica):
  implementing measures, competent-authority information and interim registers
  of authorized service providers and other entities. A register entry must be
  checked against the actual legal person and permitted activity. The page also
  states that listed cryptoasset white papers are not reviewed/approved by a
  competent authority merely because they appear in that register.
- [ESMA final report on qualification of cryptoassets as financial instruments](https://www.esma.europa.eu/sites/default/files/2024-12/ESMA75453128700-1323_Final_Report_Guidelines_on_the_conditions_and_criteria_for_the_qualification_of_CAs_as_FIs.pdf),
  linked from that official MiCA page. Have counsel use the currently applicable
  final guidance and national implementation when classifying the offering.
- [Fund management](https://www.esma.europa.eu/esmas-activities/investors-and-issuers/fund-management):
  describes UCITS and AIFMD as separate fund-management frameworks and the
  regulated scope of applicable passporting mechanisms. It does not establish
  that this offering qualifies under either framework.

These references frame questions for retained counsel. They do not replace
current consolidated legislation, local legal research, verification of actual
permissions or a signed jurisdiction-specific opinion.
