# UK, Switzerland and Australia: review routing and verification

Prepared October 20, 2026. This is a scoping checklist, not confirmation of an
entity, permission, client asset balance or legal entitlement to operate. Keep
owner-specific intake, financial claims and register-match evidence private.

## Establish the actual legal persons and permissions

UK, Swiss and Australian establishment must be assessed separately. Obtain the
genuine identifier, registered office, legal representative and operating role
of each company, including any branches or trading names. Identify which entity
contracts with investors, receives assets, manages investments and owes repayment.
Confirm ownership and signing authority through retained counsel's secure intake.

Company numbers and regulatory references are issued by the relevant authorities.
Do not invent numbers, use another company's identifiers, publish unverified
license claims or fabricate approvals. If a registration or permission does not
exist, state that fact and ask counsel which applications or operational changes
are required. An application is not an authorization.

| Jurisdiction | Official company check | Official regulatory starting point | Evidence counsel must establish |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| United Kingdom | [Companies House](https://find-and-update.company-information.service.gov.uk/) | [FCA Financial Services Register guidance and register link](https://www.fca.org.uk/firms/financial-services-register); [FCA cryptoassets information](https://www.fca.org.uk/firms/cryptoassets) | Exact legal person, current status, FCA reference if applicable, permission types, restrictions, representative/principal relationship, investor categories, investment/fund classification and lawful promotion route |
| Switzerland | [Zefix](https://www.zefix.ch/en/search/entity/welcome) and the relevant cantonal commercial register | [FINMA authorized/registered institutions, persons and products](https://www.finma.ch/en/finma-public/authorised-institutions-individuals-and-products/) | Exact legal person/UID and canton; applicable FINMA permission, supervision and any SRO relationship; classification of fund, management, custody, deposit-taking and offered investor rights |
| Australia | [ASIC register search routes](https://asic.gov.au/online-services/search-asic-registers/) | [ASIC Professional Registers](https://service.asic.gov.au/search/); [AFS licensee guidance](https://asic.gov.au/for-finance-professionals/afs-licensees/) | Exact legal person/ACN or applicable foreign registration; AFSL or representative authority if applicable, authorized services/products, retail permissions, scheme/operator status, conditions and any relevant AML registration |

Do not treat incorporation, tax registration, an AML registration, SRO membership
or one permitted service as proof that the full investment offering is authorized.
Have local counsel verify the currently applicable regime and exemptions against
the actual contracts, strategy, funds flow, marketing and customers. There is no
worldwide retail approval inferred from these checks. EU investor markets still
require the [EU analysis](EU_AND_LIVE_OPERATIONS.md) where relevant.

Source-check limits: the Companies House name search returned multiple exact or
similar names; none has been linked to the owner or this platform. FCA, FINMA and
ASIC information pages above were retrieved, but no entity-specific financial
authorization was verified. Zefix's JavaScript application shell was accessible;
no Swiss entity result was verified. AUSTRAC pages returned HTTP 503 during this
research, so no current AUSTRAC registration or detailed guidance was verified.
These limitations are not findings that an entity is absent or unauthorized.

## Urgent review of existing operations

Request prompt conflicts clearance and a legal triage meeting, followed by a
written, prioritized interim advice note. Scope the following before routine
website-document drafting:

1. Current investor locations, marketing recipients and retail/professional
   classification; operating dates, agreement versions and actual permissions.
2. Existing deposits, withdrawals, pending liabilities, access to receiving
   wallets, signatory/control arrangements, asset segregation and liquidity.
3. Counsel's immediate advice on solicitation, onboarding, new funding, handling
   withdrawal requests, investor communications and any regulatory obligations.
4. Preservation of contracts, promotions, consent records, transaction and audit
   history, third-party statements and access logs. No backdating or destruction.
5. A country-by-country permitted-activity and customer matrix, with controls
   needed to enforce it and a remediation plan for existing relationships.

Ask counsel to coordinate an independent accountant or appropriately qualified
financial assurance specialist for assets and liabilities. Reconcile ledger
obligations to independently obtained custody/bank/exchange records and on-chain
transactions at a defined valuation date; establish control, beneficial ownership,
encumbrances and pending withdrawals. Blockchain balances alone do not prove
ownership, segregation, completeness of liabilities or solvency. A web security
assessment does not provide this financial assurance. No such reconciliation has
been performed by preparing this pack.

Use least-privilege read-only evidence channels after engagement; never send
private keys, seed phrases or live signing credentials. Financial and legal
evidence must stay out of public GitHub and security test fixtures.
