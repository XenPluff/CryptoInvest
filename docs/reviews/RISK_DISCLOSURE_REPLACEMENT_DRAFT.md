# Proposal-based risk disclosure replacement

The owner confirmed that the entities, institutional products, audits, reserve
arrangements and registrations are proposed. After first requesting a status
label, the owner asked to remove that label. The document has no status label
or notice banner. Its body continues to describe the planned arrangements with
conditional wording rather than asserting current registrations, issuance,
reserve backing or audits. No supporting registration, audit or reserve records
were supplied or inferred.

## Implemented scope

- Replace the eight numbered sections with the revised multi-sector disclosure.
- Omit the risk page's notice banner, as subsequently requested by the owner;
  preserve the default notice on terms, privacy and AML. Omit the subsequently
  requested-and-removed document-header status label as well.
- State explicitly, as requested in the later revision, that investments are
  not bank deposits and are not insured or guaranteed by any government agency,
  financial protection corporation or central banking authority.
- Describe affiliated entities, permissions, custody arrangements, restricted
  offerings, AUREON Ventures, institutional audits and fund structures as plans.
- Describe AUDU, its issuer and reserve structure conditionally; do not imply a
  currently available or reserve-backed instrument.
- Describe AUREON Group LLC and trademark/service-mark registrations as plans.
  Use “© 2026 AUREON” without attributing ownership to an unestablished LLC.
- Retain the supplied version identifier, AUR-2026.04.1, and risk language about
  total loss, illiquidity, variable protection and non-guaranteed returns.
- Keep target counters distinct from posted realized earnings and available funds.
- Translate the replacement into the site's seven non-English languages.
- Present the disclosure in a formal framed document with a double gold border,
  centered brand/title, generous paragraph spacing and a version/copyright footer.
  The layout identifies a risk disclosure and does not represent a certification.

The copy is implemented in [the risk page](../../app/legal/risk/page.tsx).
Deployment and validation status are recorded in [the live preview record](../LIVE_PREVIEW.md)
and [validation report](../VALIDATION.md).

## Any future change from proposed to established

Statements of actual coverage, registration, audits, eligibility restrictions
or reserve backing should identify the specific entity/product, jurisdiction,
scope, conditions and verifiable supporting records. Software branding and
ledger functionality do not establish these external facts.
