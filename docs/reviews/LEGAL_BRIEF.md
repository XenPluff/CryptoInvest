Status: draft intake and scope for qualified counsel, not legal advice. No
operating country, investor country, licensed legal practitioner, or lawful
offering structure is assumed. AUREON is a working platform name; the contracting
entity and authorized representative remain to be confirmed by the owner.

Use the [EU/live-operations addendum](EU_AND_LIVE_OPERATIONS.md) where relevant.
Confirm the exact member state and existing investor activity privately before
selecting local counsel or framing an already-launched offering as a future one.

## Business facts counsel must establish

Obtain the operator's entity, operating locations, management/beneficial owners,
proposed investor countries and categories, marketing channels, referral terms,
investment strategy, sources of capital and yield, counterparties, custody and
wallet-control arrangements, segregation, accounting, liquidity, redemption and
fee policies. Use counsel's secure intake for supporting records and conflict
checks. Do not put confidential intake or privileged advice in this repository.

## Required legal analysis and deliverables

| Workstream | Requested written output |
| --- | --- |
| Regulatory perimeter | Classification of the actual arrangement in each relevant jurisdiction: investment/securities/fund or collective scheme, management/advice, brokerage, custody, cryptoasset service or money transmission/payment activity, as applicable; identify responsible entities and required registrations/authorizations or defensible exemptions |
| Investor eligibility and marketing | Country-by-country decision for public/retail versus restricted investors; permitted solicitation channels; financial-promotion approval, risk/suitability/appropriateness or investor-status requirements; enforceable onboarding and territorial controls |
| Return and ownership claims | Whether any proposed target rate, calculator, income language, testimonials, referral claim, or the name Shareholder can be used; evidence required; rights actually granted; prohibited or misleading representations to remove |
| Custody, segregation and insolvency | Legal ownership of deposited assets, authority to deploy them, client-money/asset obligations, wallet control, segregation, reconciliation, counterparty exposure, insurance representations, creditor priority and loss allocation |
| Financial crime | Applicable AML/CFT, identity, beneficial-owner, source-of-funds, sanctions, ongoing monitoring, reporting, Travel Rule and recordkeeping obligations; approved operational procedures and accountable personnel where required |
| Contracts and redemptions | Offering/subscription documents, investor rights, fees, valuation, losses, withdrawals and liquidity limits, complaint/dispute process, governing law and material-change/closure arrangements |
| Privacy and international processing | Controller identity, lawful bases, sensitive/identity data, processor contracts and locations, cross-border safeguards, retention, rights handling, breach response and marketing consent |
| Other specialists | Identify needed local tax, accounting, custody or financial-administration advice; distinguish these matters from the scope of the legal opinion |

Request a dated, reasoned written memorandum and a country/investor-category
matrix with statuses such as prohibited, requires authorization, permitted only
under stated conditions, or unresolved. Include the legal basis, assumptions,
limits, required actions, responsible party and remaining evidence for each
conclusion. Obtain named, locally admitted counsel or documented local-counsel
coordination; an international firm's brand does not establish local admission.

Have counsel identify whether the proposed business can proceed at all before
spending on final documents or treating website disclaimers as an exemption.
Then commission approved contracts, offering and risk disclosures, privacy/AML
documents, marketing rules and territorial controls that match the permitted
structure. Approval in one country must not be presented as global permission.

## Specific current-copy issues to review

- Terms currently leave the contracting entity, registered address, governing
  law, disputes, fees, complaint process and some eligibility rules incomplete.
- Privacy copy still describes Resend as planned transactional mail; the
  recorded deployment uses Brevo. The provider/data-flow disclosure must match
  actual processing, including hosting, email, monitoring and future backups.
- The exceptionally high target rates require substantiation and regulatory
  analysis. There is no published independently verified strategy, track record
  or return-generating asset evidence in the reviewed source materials.
- The Shareholder name needs review against actual rights; software plan naming
  does not itself issue or document ownership interests.
- Identity-form approval must not be represented as completed independent KYC,
  sanctions clearance, or legal compliance. Referral wording must match actual
  commission mechanics and any applicable promotion restrictions.
